# UK organizations fixture

A small slice of real data for tests (design 07): GLEIF Golden Copy Level 1 and 2 records registered with Companies House, and the Companies House records of those companies and their live near-namesakes.

Contains GLEIF Golden Copy data (CC0 1.0) and Companies House data, public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Built from: BasicCompanyDataAsOneFile-2026-10-01.zip (companies_house), 20261009-0800-gleif-goldencopy-lei2-golden-copy.csv.zip (lei2), 20261009-0800-gleif-goldencopy-rr-golden-copy.csv.zip (rr).
